# Design Haven Studio

Interior Design Website Build Prompt for Lovable

Project Overview

Create a professional, modern, and conversion-focused website for an independent interior design firm starting their own company in South Africa (Johannesburg/Pretoria area). The site should serve residential clients looking for full-service interior design, styling, and space transformation services.

Core Website Goals

Showcase Portfolio — Display the designer's best interior design work with stunning photography

Build Credibility — Establish the designer as a professional, experienced interior design expert

Generate Client Inquiries — Make it easy for potential clients to request consultations and book services

Tell Design Stories — Help visitors emotionally connect with the work through lifestyle imagery and narratives

Mobile-First Experience — Ensure 68% of traffic (mobile users) have a seamless experience

Website Structure & Pages

1. Homepage / Landing Page

Design Philosophy: Let the work speak for itself. Portfolio-first approach.

Key Elements:

Hero Section:

Full-width, high-quality image carousel of 3-4 signature projects (before/after or completed spaces)

Brief tagline (e.g., "Transforming Homes, Creating Lifestyles" or "Interior Design That Reflects Who You Are")

Subtle call-to-action button: "Book a Consultation" or "View Our Projects"

Keep text minimal; visuals are the hero

Featured Projects Grid:

6-8 thumbnail project images in a clean grid layout

On hover, reveal project name and style category (e.g., "Modern Minimalist," "Luxury Contemporary")

Clickable to full project detail pages

Ensure responsive: vertical scrolling on mobile, multi-column grid on desktop

Designer Introduction Section:

Professional headshot of the designer

Brief, warm bio (2-3 sentences) about design philosophy and approach

Link to full About page

Tone: approachable, personable, professional

Why Choose This Designer:

3-4 key differentiators (e.g., "Custom, personalized approach," "Sustainable material focus," "Full project management")

Short, benefit-focused statements

Consider using icons to visual support each point

Testimonials Preview:

2-3 client testimonials with headshots/names

Short quotes (1-2 sentences) emphasizing transformation and satisfaction

Star ratings (5 stars ideal)

Call-to-Action Section:

"Ready to transform your space?"

Contact form or "Book a Free Consultation" button

Clear messaging about next steps

2. Portfolio / Projects Page

Design Philosophy: This is the most important page. High-quality imagery takes center stage.

Key Elements:

Portfolio Grid:

Minimum 12-25 curated projects (only include work that represents the type of clients you want to attract next)

Large, high-resolution images

Hover effect: reveal project name, room type, style category, and "View Project" CTA

Filter options (optional but recommended):

By style: Modern, Luxury, Contemporary, Minimalist, etc.

By space type: Bedroom, Living Room, Kitchen, Bathroom, Full Home, Commercial

By specialty: Residential, Commercial, Hospitality

Project Detail Pages (click-through):

Full-width hero image of the main space

Project title, location (city), and design style/category

5-12 high-quality images showing:

Overall room views

Different angles and zones

Detail shots of finishes, textures, materials

Lifestyle photography (if available) showing the space in use with people/furnishings

Before & after comparisons (extremely powerful for showing transformation)

Project Narrative/Description:

Client challenge (what was the problem/opportunity?)

Design solution (how was it addressed?)

Key materials & finishes used

Design inspiration or concept

Project timeline / scope (if relevant)

Tone: storytelling, not overly technical; help reader envision themselves in the space

Design Details Section:

Color palette (visual color swatches with hex codes or names)

Key materials used (flooring, wall treatments, textiles, furnishings)

Any custom or bespoke elements

Sustainability notes if applicable (eco-friendly materials, energy efficiency)

Related Projects:

3-4 "Related Projects" section at bottom showing similar style/space type

Encourages browsing and extends time on site

3. About / About the Designer Page

Design Philosophy: Build personal connection and credibility.

Key Elements:

Designer Bio:

Professional headshot (warm, approachable)

Full biography (150-200 words)

Design philosophy and approach to spaces

What makes this designer unique

Personal touch: a bit about design inspiration, style influences, or what drives the work

Credentials & Experience:

Years in practice / industry experience

Formal education / certifications (if applicable)

Professional memberships (IIDA, ASID, local design bodies, etc.)

Awards or media features (if applicable)

Design Approach / Philosophy Section:

Clear explanation of how this designer works

Values: sustainability, personalization, functionality, aesthetics, etc.

Visual diagram or step-by-step breakdown of the design process is helpful

Team (if applicable):

Photos and bios of team members

Roles and specializations

Adds credibility and shows professionalism

Testimonial Block:

3-5 full client testimonials

Include client name, photo if possible, and what they say about working with the designer

Focus on transformation, communication, and satisfaction

4. Services / What We Offer Page

Design Philosophy: Clear, benefit-focused descriptions of services available.

Key Elements:

Service Categories (examples; customize to actual offerings):

Full Interior Design: End-to-end design from concept to installation (include timeline, scope, investment range if comfortable)

Space Planning & Layout: Room redesign, furniture arrangement, flow optimization

Styling & Curation: Accessorizing, refreshing existing spaces, curated shopping

Color Consultation: Color palette selection and application

Sustainability-Focused Design: Eco-friendly materials, energy-conscious choices

Commercial/Hospitality Design: If offered

Project Management: Coordination with contractors, vendors, installation

For Each Service:

Brief description of what it includes

Who it's ideal for (what kind of client)

Key benefits

Visual icon or supporting image

Call-to-action: "Learn More" or "Book Consultation"

Process / How It Works Section:

Step-by-step breakdown of a typical project:

Initial consultation

Design concept & presentation

Refinement & feedback

Final implementation

Reveal/handoff

Visual timeline or infographic

Expected duration for each phase

Investment & Packages (optional):

If comfortable sharing, outline service packages and approximate investment ranges

e.g., "Styling packages from R5,000," "Full design from R25,000"

Or simply: "Investment varies based on project scope. We work with all budgets."

Consultation CTA:

"Every project starts with a conversation. Book your free consultation today."

Simple inquiry form or contact button

5. Contact / Get Started Page

Design Philosophy: Make it effortless to reach out and start the process.

Key Elements:

Contact Form:

Fields: Name, Email, Phone, Project Type (dropdown: Bedroom, Kitchen, Full Home, etc.), Budget Range (optional), Message/Project Brief

Clear submit button

Confirmation message after submission

Form should be visible above the fold on mobile

Contact Information:

Email address

Phone number

Physical address (if showing an office/studio location in Johannesburg/Pretoria)

Business hours

Response time expectation (e.g., "We respond within 24 hours")

Inquiry Process / Next Steps:

Explain what happens after they submit:

Initial consultation call (30-60 min) to discuss vision, budget, timeline

Project proposal and design retainer agreement

Design process begins

This reduces friction and sets expectations

Social Links:

Instagram, Pinterest, LinkedIn, etc.

These are important discovery channels for interior designers

Location Map (if applicable):

Embedded Google Map showing studio location in Johannesburg/Pretoria area

Directions link

Newsletter Signup (optional):

"Get design inspiration and tips delivered to your inbox"

Build email list for future marketing

Design & Visual Guidelines

Color Palette & Aesthetics

Inspiration: Arch-Live Architects & Interior Designers (South African luxury residential aesthetic) + high-end interior design firms

Use a sophisticated, muted color scheme:

Primary: Neutral whites, soft grays, warm taupes, creams

Accent colors: Deep jewel tones (emerald, navy, terracotta) OR warm earth tones depending on design style

Modern, minimalist approach with generous whitespace

Avoid busy patterns; let photography dominate

Typography

Clean, modern sans-serif fonts for navigation and headers (e.g., Inter, Proxima Nova, or similar)

Elegant serif font for project titles / section headers (e.g., Playfair Display, Lora) if lifestyle-focused

Body text: Easy to read, sufficient contrast, 16px+ on mobile

Typography should feel premium but approachable

Photography & Imagery

High-resolution, professional-quality photos are non-negotiable

Large, full-width images on desktop

Vertical scrolling priority for mobile (images stack naturally)

Include lifestyle photography with people/furniture in spaces when possible (shows spaces as lived-in, not sterile)

Consistent photo editing/color grading across portfolio (cohesive aesthetic)

Before & after images are powerful conversion tools

Layout & Navigation

Minimal navigation menu: Home, Portfolio, About, Services, Contact (5 items max)

Sticky header on scroll (nav always accessible)

Clear visual hierarchy (large images, minimal text)

Ample whitespace between sections

Generous padding/margins to feel premium

Mobile-first responsive design: touch-friendly buttons (44px+ targets), vertical scrolling, collapsed menus

Interaction & User Experience

Smooth scroll behavior

Subtle hover effects on project thumbnails (slight zoom, overlay with project name)

Fast page load times (optimize images with WebP format, lazy loading)

Smooth transitions between pages

Clear visual feedback on buttons and forms

Content Requirements & Tone

Tone of Voice

Professional but approachable: Not overly formal, but credible

Client-focused: Emphasize benefits and transformation, not just aesthetics

Warm and personable: This is a personal service; let personality shine through

Inspiring: Imagery and language should evoke emotion and aspiration

Clear and concise: Avoid design jargon; speak to homeowners, not just designers

Key Messaging

"Transforming spaces, creating homes that reflect who you are"

Emphasize personalization, listening, and understanding client vision

Highlight attention to detail, quality materials, and expert execution

Mention full-service approach from concept to completion

If applicable: sustainability, local partnerships, support for local artisans

Project Descriptions (for Portfolio)

Focus on the transformation story

Answer: What was the challenge? What was the solution? What materials/colors were used? How does the space feel now?

Use sensory language ("warm," "inviting," "sophisticated," "calming")

100-150 words per project is ideal

Include a "Key Elements" section listing main materials, colors, and design decisions

Instead of: "Full design service." → "From your vision to your dream space—we handle every detail so you can just enjoy the transformation."

Final Instruction for Lovable AI

Build a professional, modern interior design website that prioritizes stunning photography and storytelling. The site should feel premium and professional while remaining warm and approachable. Navigation should be intuitive and minimal. Mobile experience is critical. The portfolio is the star; minimize text and maximize high-quality imagery. Every interaction should guide potential clients toward requesting a consultation. Ensure all design choices reflect the sophistication and attention to detail expected from a professional interior designer.
